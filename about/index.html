About VPL Ortho and Spine Clinic

Our Vision for Public Service

At VPL Ortho and Spine Clinic, we are committed to providing accessible, high-quality orthopedic and spine care to the community of Coimbatore and surrounding regions. Our vision is to ensure that every patient receives personalized, compassionate treatment that restores mobility and improves quality of life.

We believe in:

  • Accessibility: Making expert orthopedic care available to all segments of society
  • Affordability: Providing cost-effective treatment options without compromising on quality
  • Awareness: Educating the public about preventive measures and early intervention for orthopedic conditions
  • Excellence: Delivering the highest standard of medical care using advanced surgical techniques

About Dr. Vijaya Anand S

Dr. Vijaya Anand S is a Consultant Orthopaedic and Spine Surgeon with extensive experience in treating a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions. With a passion for patient care and a commitment to surgical excellence, Dr. Vijaya Anand S has helped numerous patients regain their mobility and lead pain-free lives.

Expertise

  • Joint Replacement Surgery (Knee, Hip, Shoulder)
  • Spine Surgery and Interventions
  • Trauma and Fracture Management
  • Sports Injuries
  • Arthroscopic Procedures
  • Pediatric Orthopedics

Commitment to Community

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Vijaya Anand S is dedicated to public health awareness. Through this clinic's blog and community outreach programs, we aim to educate people about:

  • Preventive measures for common orthopedic problems
  • Importance of early diagnosis and treatment
  • Lifestyle modifications for joint and spine health
  • Post-surgery rehabilitation and recovery
