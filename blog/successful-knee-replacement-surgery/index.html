Successful Knee Replacement Surgery - Patient Recovery Update

By Dr. Vijaya Anand on 2026-04-10

Surgery Case

We recently performed a successful total knee replacement surgery on a 65-year-old patient suffering from severe osteoarthritis. The patient had been experiencing chronic knee pain and limited mobility for several years, affecting their daily activities and quality of life.

Procedure Details

The surgery was performed using advanced minimally invasive techniques, which resulted in:

  • Smaller incision
  • Reduced blood loss
  • Faster recovery time
  • Less post-operative pain

Recovery Progress

  • Day 1: Patient started walking with assistance
  • Day 3: Discharged from hospital
  • Week 2: Walking independently without support
  • Week 4: Started physiotherapy exercises
  • Week 8: Near-normal range of motion achieved

The patient is now able to perform daily activities without pain and has resumed their morning walks. This case demonstrates how modern orthopedic techniques can significantly improve quality of life for patients with severe joint degeneration.

If you're experiencing chronic knee pain or mobility issues, schedule a consultation with us to explore your treatment options.
